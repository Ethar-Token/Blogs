Privacy Policy

We believe visitors should understand what information may be collected when they use our website and how that information is handled.

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In brief: We Said It is an article-based website. You can browse our published content without creating an account. Like most websites, certain technical information may be processed when you visit, including information handled by our hosting and website service providers.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how We Said It ("we", "us", "our" or "the website") handles information when you visit or use our website.

We Said It is an independent blog that publishes articles covering human behaviour, relationships, dating, gossip, internet culture, unusual experiences and opinions.

This policy applies to our website and its published content. It does not automatically apply to external websites or services that we link to.

2. Information We May Collect

The information processed when you visit our website depends on how you interact with it and which website services are active.

Technical and usage information

Our hosting provider and other technical service providers may automatically process certain information when your browser requests a page. This may include:

  • IP address and general network information.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Device type and operating system.
  • Pages requested and approximate visit times.
  • Referring pages and basic diagnostic information.

This information may be used to deliver website pages, maintain security, troubleshoot technical problems and protect the website against misuse.

Information you provide

If you contact us directly by email or another available communication channel, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, name and the contents of your message.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is genuinely necessary for your enquiry.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed through the website may be used for purposes such as:

  • Making our website and articles available to visitors.
  • Maintaining website performance and reliability.
  • Identifying and resolving technical errors.
  • Protecting the website against spam, abuse and security threats.
  • Responding to enquiries sent directly to us.
  • Understanding website usage where analytics services are enabled.
  • Meeting applicable legal requirements.

We do not sell visitors' personal information as part of our ordinary website operations.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. Similar technologies can also be used to remember preferences or understand website activity.

Depending on the services enabled on our website, cookies or similar technologies may be used for:

  • Essential website functions and security.
  • Remembering certain preferences.
  • Website analytics and performance measurement.
  • Advertising and measuring advertising performance, if advertising services are enabled.

Third-party services integrated into the website may use their own cookies or similar technologies under their respective privacy policies.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect how some website features work.

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, we will seek that consent before using them.

5. Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party providers to support the operation and presentation of our website. These may include:

  • Website hosting: Our website is published through GitHub Pages. Technical information may be processed as part of hosting, delivery and security operations.
  • Domain and network services: Services such as Cloudflare may support domain management, DNS or website security and delivery.
  • Fonts and visual resources: Google Fonts and Font Awesome may provide fonts and icons used in the website interface.
  • Analytics or advertising: If we introduce analytics or advertising providers, those services may process information according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements.

Third-party providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage visitors to review those policies when using services provided by others.

We do not control the privacy practices of external websites or services.

6. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed or disclosed where reasonably necessary:

  • To hosting, infrastructure and technical service providers operating the website.
  • To service providers whose tools we choose to integrate.
  • To comply with applicable laws, regulations or lawful requests.
  • To protect the rights, security and integrity of the website and others.

Where third parties process information on our behalf, their access should be limited to what is necessary for the relevant service.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed through our website against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

These measures may include using reputable service providers, limiting access to administrative systems and maintaining appropriate technical safeguards.

No website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security.

8. How Long Information Is Kept

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Technical logs may be retained by hosting or infrastructure providers according to their own retention practices.

If you contact us, we may retain correspondence for as long as needed to respond, maintain relevant records or address a legal matter.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable data protection laws, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These rights may include:

  • The right to know whether and how your personal information is processed.
  • The right to request access to personal information held about you.
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • The right to request deletion where applicable.
  • The right to object to certain processing.
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a relevant data protection authority.

If you are in Kenya, you may have rights under the Data Protection Act, 2019, subject to its requirements and applicable exceptions.

You can find information about the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at www.odpc.go.ke .

To make a privacy-related request to us, use the contact details provided below.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

11. International Visitors

We Said It may be accessed by visitors from different countries. Our hosting and other service providers may process technical information in locations outside your country of residence.

Where applicable data protection laws regulate international transfers, we aim to use appropriate safeguards required by those laws.

12. External Links

Our articles or pages may contain links to third-party websites, services or resources.

Once you leave We Said It, the privacy policy and practices of the external website will apply. We are not responsible for the content, security or privacy practices of third-party websites.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, information-handling practices or applicable laws.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically to stay informed about how information is handled.
