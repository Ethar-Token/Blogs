About We Said It

A place for the stories, opinions, behaviours and everyday experiences that make people stop, think, laugh, question and sometimes say, "I thought I was the only one."

What Is We Said It?

We Said It is an independent blog exploring the fascinating, funny, complicated and sometimes unbelievable sides of everyday life.

From unusual human habits and relationship experiences to modern dating, gossip, internet culture and unpopular opinions, we publish articles about subjects people are curious about but don't always know how to bring up.

Some stories are entertaining. Others are thought-provoking, surprising or relatable. What connects them is our interest in the things people do, think, experience and sometimes keep to themselves.

Why We Said It Exists

Everyday life is full of moments worth talking about. Sometimes they're funny. Sometimes they're uncomfortable. Sometimes they make us question what we thought we knew about people.

We Said It exists to explore those moments through interesting articles, relatable observations and different perspectives.

Our goal is simple: to publish content that makes people curious, gives them something to think about and keeps them coming back for another read.

Our Approach to Content

We aim to make our articles engaging, clear and enjoyable to read. We value originality, thoughtful presentation and content that gives readers a reason to keep exploring.

Our articles may include commentary, opinions, observations and discussions of real-life situations. Where a topic involves facts or advice, readers should consider the context and seek qualified guidance when appropriate.

We are a blog, not a professional counselling, medical or legal service. Our content is intended for general information, entertainment and discussion.
